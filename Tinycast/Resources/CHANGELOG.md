# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Clipboard history retention: keep entries for 1 day, 1 week, 1 month or 3 months (replaces the item-count limit).
- Disabled applications: clipboard changes from listed apps are never recorded (Keychain Access and Passwords by default).
- The Clipboard History shortcut now lives in the Clipboard settings tab.
- Settings window polish: tighter pane headers and a full-height sidebar separator.

## 0.1.0

- App launcher with fuzzy search.
- Clipboard history with text and image preview.
- Global and per-app hotkeys.
- Favorites and running-app indicators.
